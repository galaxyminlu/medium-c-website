Services

Everything you need to show up well online, handled properly

You can bring us in for one thing or all of it. Most clients start with one service and add others once they see how we work.

Social media management

We plan, write, design and schedule your posts across the platforms that matter to your business, whether that's Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or a mix, including branded graphics and Reels. That includes a proper monthly content calendar and adjustments when something isn't landing. You'll always know what's going out before it does.

Content & copywriting

Blog posts, website copy, case studies, educational content and thought leadership written in a voice that sounds like your business, researched properly rather than padded out with filler. Useful both for building trust with visitors and for search engines.

Email marketing

Newsletters, campaigns and promotional EDMs that keep your business front of mind between visits, plus simple automations for welcome sequences and follow-ups so nothing falls through the cracks.

Website content

Clear, persuasive website copy designed for both people and search. I can help with landing pages, service pages, homepage updates, blogs and ongoing website content, with basic keyword research and SEO best practices built in.

For more technical SEO requirements, I can work alongside your SEO specialist or developer.

Digital campaigns

Planning and running campaigns across the channels that make sense for you, including Meta, LinkedIn, email and web, all built around a clear goal, whether that's leads, sales or simply getting seen.

Marketing strategy & reporting

A content and campaign plan built around your actual goals and busy periods, backed by a monthly summary that tells you plainly what worked, what didn't and what's changing next.

How we get started

A simple, low-friction process

  1. A first conversation

    We talk through where your marketing stands today, what's worked in the past, and what you'd actually like to achieve. No obligation, no hard sell.

  2. A proposal you can actually read

    A short, clear outline of what we'd do, how often, and what it costs. No confusing tiers or hidden extras.

  3. Onboarding

    We learn your business properly, brand voice, past content, key services, and the details that matter to your customers.

  4. Ongoing work, with regular check-ins

    Content goes out on a steady rhythm, with a monthly report and a standing invitation to call if anything needs adjusting sooner.
