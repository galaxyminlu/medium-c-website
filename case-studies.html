Engineering Social media management

A structural engineering firm making its expertise visible online

The situation. A structural engineering firm won most of its work through referrals and reputation, but had little presence online to back that up. When a prospective client or partner looked them up, there was nothing recent to see, which undercut the strong word-of-mouth that was bringing people to them in the first place.

What we did. We took over social media management across LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook, turning real project work into a steady stream of posts that showed the firm's capability. The goal wasn't chasing followers; it was making sure that when someone checked the firm out after a referral, they found an active, credible presence that matched the reputation.

The result. The firm now has a professional, consistent presence that validates the referrals coming its way, so a strong recommendation is backed up by what people find when they look. Reporting each month tracks reach and engagement across all three platforms and keeps the content focused on what's actually moving.

Ongoing
Focus

Outcome
Credibility that backs referrals
EV & clean energy Multi-channel campaign

An EV charging company that turned a seasonal sale into its biggest revenue period

The situation. An electric vehicle charging company running several brands needed a standout result for its biggest sales period of the year. Previous seasonal pushes had been handled piecemeal, without a single person owning the whole campaign end to end, so the channels weren't working together.

What we did. We planned and ran the entire campaign across every channel: a sequence of emails to the customer base, supporting paid social on Meta, organic social content, and a website pop-up to capture on-site visitors. Everything was built, scheduled, and optimised as it ran, with daily monitoring to adjust targeting and send times based on what the data showed.

The result. The three-week campaign generated over $600,000 in revenue, making it the company's strongest sales period. Because one person owned the full campaign, the channels reinforced each other instead of competing, and the approach became a repeatable template for future campaigns.

Timeframe
3 weeks
Focus
Email, paid social & web
Outcome
$600K+ in revenue
Property Social media & content

A buyer's agency building a consistent presence in a competitive market

The situation. A buyer's agency operating in a crowded property market needed to stand out and stay visible to potential clients who were researching online before making contact. Their social presence was inconsistent, and their content wasn't reflecting the expertise the team brought to every purchase.

What we did. We developed a steady content rhythm that translated the agency's buyer-side knowledge into posts people actually found useful, covering the kinds of questions buyers ask before they commit. The focus was on building credibility and keeping the agency front of mind, with content that sounded like the team rather than generic property filler.

The result. The agency moved from sporadic posting to a consistent, professional presence that reinforced its expertise and gave potential clients a reason to trust them before the first conversation. The content gave the team something credible to point to when prospects were weighing up who to work with.

Ongoing
Focus
Social & content strategy
Outcome
Consistent, credible presence
